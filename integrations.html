External account connections

Connection framework is the next step

This project has OAuth app registration for other apps to connect to ConfigKit, but it does not yet have the separate outbound OAuth flow needed for ConfigKit to connect to Google, Microsoft, GitHub, Slack or other services. I’ve kept those providers clearly unconnected rather than showing fake “Connected” switches. Each provider needs its own OAuth client configuration, secure token storage, scopes and disconnect/revoke handling.

No external account credentials or access tokens are stored in this page.
