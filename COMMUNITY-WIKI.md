# ConfigKit Community Wiki

## What it is
The Community Wiki is a member-written knowledge library separate from the official ConfigKit Help Centre (wiki.html). Published articles are publicly readable. Signing in is required to create, edit, publish, archive, and report articles.

## Member features
- Browse and search published articles by title, summary, category, and body text.
- Write Markdown-style articles with headings, lists, blockquotes, and fenced code blocks.
- Save private drafts, publish articles, edit own articles, archive published articles, and delete drafts/archived entries.
- Report an article for unsafe content, spam, harassment, copyright, misinformation, or another concern.
- Article content is rendered as text and safe DOM nodes; raw HTML is not executed.

## Content limits and guidelines
- Title: 4–140 characters.
- Summary: up to 500 characters.
- Category: 2–48 characters.
- Body: 80–30,000 characters.
- Slugs are unique and generated by the page.
- Do not publish credentials, recovery codes, personal secrets, private information, unsafe instructions, harassment, or material you do not have rights to republish.
- Public articles can be hidden by the ConfigKit site administrator. Hidden content is not visible to readers and its author cannot edit or restore it.

## Moderation
The registered ConfigKit site owner reviews reports in owner-control.html. The owner can hide and restore articles and update report status/notes. Reports are private to the reporter and the registered site owner.

## Database
Migration: supabase/migrations/20261120100047_create_community_wiki.sql

Tables:
- public.community_wiki_pages — article body, author, status, category, generated search vector.
- public.community_wiki_reports — private reports and moderation status.

Both tables have RLS enabled. Anonymous users can select only published, visible articles. Signed-in users can read published visible articles and their own entries. Authors can manage only their own drafts/published/archived entries. The registered site administrator has a separate trusted authorization path through private.configkit_is_site_admin(). Reports can only be submitted by signed-in users and can only be reviewed by the registered administrator.
