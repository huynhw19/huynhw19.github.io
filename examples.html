More mobileconfig examples

Browse common Apple configuration payloads and adapt the snippets for learning or prototyping.

Important: These are illustrative payload fragments, not complete installable profiles. A real profile needs the outer configuration dictionary, unique identifiers and UUIDs, and may require signing, device enrollment, supervision, or administrator approval. Check Apple's current documentation and test on a non-production device before installing.

Wi-Fi network

Join a WPA2/WPA3 Personal network automatically. Replace the sample SSID and password; keep credentials private.

<dict>
  <key>PayloadType</key><string>com.apple.wifi.managed</string>
  <key>PayloadVersion</key><integer>1</integer>
  <key>SSID_STR</key><string>Example Network</string>
  <key>AutoJoin</key><true/>
  <key>EncryptionType</key><string>WPA</string>
  <key>Password</key><string>replace-with-a-secret</string>
</dict>

The password is stored in the profile. Do not share a profile containing a real network password.

Home Screen web clip

Add a labeled shortcut to a website. Only use a URL you trust.

<dict>
  <key>PayloadType</key><string>com.apple.webClip.managed</string>
  <key>PayloadVersion</key><integer>1</integer>
  <key>Label</key><string>Company Portal</string>
  <key>URL</key><string>https://example.com/</string>
  <key>IsRemovable</key><true/>
  <key>FullScreen</key><false/>
</dict>

Passcode policy

A basic policy requiring a passcode of at least six characters and allowing a short auto-lock grace period.

<dict>
  <key>PayloadType</key><string>com.apple.mobiledevice.passwordpolicy</string>
  <key>PayloadVersion</key><integer>1</integer>
  <key> minLength</key><integer>6</integer>
  <key>maxFailedAttempts</key><integer>10</integer>
  <key>maxGracePeriod</key><integer>5</integer>
</dict>

Passcode policy support and behavior vary by OS version and management method. Review the keys against Apple's payload reference.

Restrictions payload

Example of a restriction setting with an explicit boolean value. Confirm availability for the target platform and OS.

<dict>
  <key>PayloadType</key><string>com.apple.applicationaccess</string>
  <key>PayloadVersion</key><integer>1</integer>
  <key>allowSafari</key><true/>
  <key>allowCamera</key><true/>
</dict>

This demonstrates the payload shape only; it does not apply a restrictive setting. Restriction keys and supervision requirements vary.

Encrypted DNS (DoH)

Configure DNS over HTTPS with a resolver URL. Substitute a resolver operated by an organization you trust.

<dict>
  <key>PayloadType</key><string>com.apple.dnsSettings.managed</string>
  <key>PayloadVersion</key><integer>1</integer>
  <key>DNSSettings</key>
  <dict>
    <key>DNSProtocol</key><string>HTTPS</string>
    <key>ServerURL</key><string>https://dns.example.net/dns-query</string>
  </dict>
</dict>

DNS settings payload support depends on Apple's OS versions and management context. Use a valid resolver endpoint.
