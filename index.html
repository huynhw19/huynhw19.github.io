7-day forecast

Melbourne current conditions use the latest available Bureau of Meteorology (BOM) Melbourne Olympic Park observations where available. Other locations use Open-Meteo model estimates. The 7-day forecast is provided by Open-Meteo. BOM observations are station readings and may differ across suburbs; rainfall is since 9 am. Forecasts can differ from local conditions.
