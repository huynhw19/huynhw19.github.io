How to turn a recipe into an Apple Shortcut

  1. Open the Apple Shortcuts app on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  2. Create a new shortcut and add the actions in the order shown in your ConfigKit recipe.
  3. Replace example values with your own URLs, prompts and app names.
  4. Test it with non-sensitive sample data before using it in an automation.
  5. Use Apple's own sharing controls in Shortcuts if you want to distribute a native shortcut.

ConfigKit does not run automations on your device or access private app data. Review every action before running a shortcut.
