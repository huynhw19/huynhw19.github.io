Getting started with ConfigKit for your organization

A practical checklist for creating profiles and reviewing them before using them with your team.

  1. Choose a profile builder. Use Mobileconfig Studio to combine supported payloads, or start with the focused Wi‑Fi, DNS, or web clip builder.
  2. Review before use. Open Prepare or inspect a profile to examine its XML and metadata. Compare settings with examples and read the safety guidance.
  3. Test carefully. Try the unsigned profile on a test device first. Confirm the payloads, network settings, and intended device behavior before distributing it.
  4. Share through approved channels. Use your organization’s established device-management and communication processes. Do not send passwords or sensitive profile contents through public channels.
  5. Keep sensitive data local and protected. ConfigKit generates profiles in your browser and does not store the generated profile XML or Wi‑Fi credentials. Treat downloaded profiles as sensitive files.
