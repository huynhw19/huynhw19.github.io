04 · Shape and layout

Use modular geometry, soft corners and consistent spacing. Avoid ornamental gradients or excessive decoration.

SpacingUse a 4px base rhythm: 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 and 48px.
Corner radius10–18px for controls and cards; larger radii for hero panels.
Icon styleSimple geometric shapes with consistent stroke weight and rounded ends.
