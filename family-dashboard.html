Getting started

Set up your family dashboard

  1. Sign in or create a personal account. Family tools are available to personal accounts.
  2. Use Add a child below to create a profile with a name. Age, school year or grade, and notes are optional.
  3. Choose Edit to update a profile or Delete to remove it.
  4. Keep notes appropriate for a private family record. Do not enter passwords or highly sensitive information.

Children use a separate PIN-protected session. Your adult account remains in control of setup and approvals.

Parent & carer controls

🔐 Family Safety & Privacy Centre

Keep child accounts private, review connections and use the existing approval and reporting tools. Kids Messages is intended for approved connections, not public contact with strangers.

  • Review pending friend requests and safety reports in the Parent Inbox.
  • Manage child profiles and PINs in Manage children.
  • Use Kids Messages Centre for adult messaging controls.
  • Review account security and sign-in options in Account settings.
  • Read the ConfigKit privacy information.
Emergency reminder: For an immediate danger or emergency, contact your local emergency services. Keep emergency contacts and highly sensitive information in a secure place intended for that purpose, not in this browser organiser.
