Getting started

Set up your family dashboard

  1. Sign in or create a personal account. Family tools are available to personal accounts.
  2. Use Add a child below to create a profile with a name. Age, school year or grade, and notes are optional.
  3. Choose Edit to update a profile or Delete to remove it.
  4. Keep notes appropriate for a private family record. Do not enter passwords or highly sensitive information.

Children use a separate PIN-protected session. Your adult account remains in control of setup and approvals.
