Backup & Restore

Create a portable copy of your ConfigKit workspace, keep it in your private cloud backup history, or restore it as a new copy when you need it.

Important: This is a personal workspace backup, not a full Supabase database snapshot. It includes selected ConfigKit content and the actual files attached to profile versions and Drive. It does not include passwords, sign-in sessions, MFA secrets, payment connections, family/child data, mail, or shared community records. Restores add new copies and never overwrite your current items.
