Security and access principles

        
    
          
  • Administrator roles are assigned outside the browser by a trusted project owner.
    • 
          
  • Role records are protected by Row Level Security; signed-in users can read only their own role assignments.
    • 
          
  • School administrators and teachers must be scoped to a school; only Super Admin has platform-wide scope.
    • 
          
  • Administrative audit entries are append-only from the browser and are associated with the signed-in actor.
    • 
          
  • Student information must be minimised, protected and shown only to authorised people.
