Getting started with ConfigKit for your enterprise

A practical checklist for creating profiles and reviewing them before using them with a team.

  1. Choose a builder. Start with Mobileconfig Studio, or use the Wi-Fi, DNS, or web clip builder.
  2. Review before use. Inspect the file with Prepare or inspect a profile and consult examples and safety guidance.
  3. Test on a non-production device. Confirm the profile settings and expected device behavior before distributing it.
  4. Share through approved channels. Use your organization's device-management process. Never share credentials or sensitive profile content through unapproved channels.
  5. Keep sensitive information protected. Profile generation happens in your browser; ConfigKit does not store generated profile XML or Wi-Fi credentials.

Account scope: Enterprise identity and organization name are self-declared profile details. This demo does not verify organizations, provision seats, manage team members, or administer devices.
