Good security habits

  • Enable an authenticator factor and keep a separate backup method.
  • Use a unique password and a passkey where supported.
  • Keep recovery codes in a password manager, not in screenshots or shared notes.
  • Review active sessions after using a shared or unfamiliar device.
  • Never share verification codes, recovery codes or setup keys with anyone.

ConfigKit does not display your password, authenticator secret, passkey private key or recovery-code values in this overview.
