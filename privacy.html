Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how the ConfigKit website (“we,” “our,” or “the site”) handles information. ConfigKit is a lightweight demo for creating basic Apple configuration profiles.

In short: Profile information you enter into the builder is processed in your browser to create a download. The builder does not send those fields to us.

Information you enter

The profile builder creates the .mobileconfig file locally in your browser. The profile name, Wi-Fi network name, security selection, and password are used by the page to generate that file; they are not intentionally transmitted to a ConfigKit server. Avoid using the builder on a shared or compromised device, and handle generated files as sensitive because they may contain your Wi-Fi password.

The sign-in, sign-up, and newsletter interfaces on the site are demonstrations only. They do not create accounts or submit your entries through the page.

Technical information and hosting

When you visit a website, its hosting provider and network infrastructure may receive basic technical information such as your IP address, browser information, and requested page as part of delivering and securing the site. This site is hosted using GitHub Pages; GitHub’s own privacy terms describe its handling of this information. We do not control the host’s infrastructure or retention practices.

We do not knowingly use the site to run advertising trackers or analytics. Your browser may store or process information according to its own settings and extensions.

Third-party services

This site may link to third-party websites or documentation. Those services have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them. Please review their terms before providing them with information.

Children

This demo is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date above indicates when it was last revised. For privacy questions, use the contact information provided by the repository or site owner.

This page is general information, not legal advice.
