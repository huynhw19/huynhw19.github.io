Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of ConfigKit (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal information when you visit or use the ConfigKit website and its features. ConfigKit is an independent website and is not affiliated with Apple.

In short: Some profile-building tasks run locally in your browser. Account, sign-in, security and community features use online services, including Supabase. Information handled by those features is not limited to your browser.

1. Australian privacy framework

We aim to handle personal information in a transparent and secure way. Where the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) apply to us, we handle personal information in accordance with those requirements. Whether the Act applies to a particular organisation can depend on its circumstances, including applicable small-business exemptions. This policy is intended to explain our practices and does not limit any rights you have under applicable law.

2. Information we may collect

The information collected depends on which features you use. It may include:

  • Account information: email address, account identifier, and profile details you choose to provide.
  • Sign-in and security information: authentication and session records, identity-provider identifiers, multi-factor authentication enrolment information, passkey credential information, recovery-code verification records, and trusted-device or session metadata. Recovery codes are designed for one-time use; the server stores verification data rather than a reusable plain-text copy of your codes.
  • Content you submit: information, text, profile details, or other material you choose to submit through account, community, discussion or related features.
  • Technical information: information that may be received by hosting, security or network services, such as IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, timestamps and diagnostic logs.
  • Support requests: the category, subject, message, page path, request status, and any support response you submit or receive through the signed-in ConfigKit Support portal.
  • Notifications: in-app notification records about selected ConfigKit activity, including social follows, discussion replies, new messages, family assignments and support updates, together with your notification preferences and read or dismissal status.
  • Messages to us: information you include when contacting the site operator.

Please do not submit sensitive information unless a feature specifically requires it and you are comfortable doing so. Do not share passwords, access tokens, authenticator secrets or unused recovery codes with anyone.

3. Profile builder information

The configuration-profile builder is designed to generate the profile file in your browser. Information entered into that builder, such as a profile name or Wi-Fi network details, is used by the page to create the file locally and is not intentionally sent to our account database by the profile-generation step. Your browser, extensions, device backups or other software may still retain information. Generated profiles can contain sensitive credentials; protect the file and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Other site features operate differently. When you sign in, save account information or use online community features, the information required for those features is transmitted to the relevant service providers.

Money tracking and payments

ConfigKit Money Centre is a manual record-keeping tool. Users enter and manage their own account labels, balances, transactions and bill reminders. ConfigKit does not connect to financial institutions, import bank statements, initiate transfers, process card payments, or hold funds. Community Store listings are free downloads only; existing historical order records are retained but no payment processing is available.

Insights and analytics

ConfigKit Insights reads existing records that are already stored for your signed-in account, including money tracker entries, personal planner items, family planner items visible to your Family ID, education assignments visible to your account, and ConfigKit Drive metadata. Insights calculates summaries in the browser using the existing database permissions and does not create a separate activity-tracking history, advertising profile, or third-party analytics event stream. Summary CSV exports are generated in your browser.

4. ConfigKit Drive

ConfigKit Drive lets signed-in users upload and organise files and folders in a private Supabase Storage bucket. Drive stores file contents with Supabase Storage and related metadata such as file names, folder relationships, file sizes, MIME types and timestamps in the ConfigKit database. Drive file access is restricted to the owning account by storage and database access policies. Files are not publicly listed or made public by default.

Users can create temporary signed links for individual files or copy a folder share list containing separate links for each file in that folder and its subfolders. Folder sharing does not create a public folder page or grant ongoing account access. Each file link expires after one hour. Anyone who receives an active link may be able to access that file, so treat links as private and only share them with people you trust. Drive currently limits individual uploads to 50 MB. Files moved to the Bin remain stored until permanently deleted; permanently deleting a file removes its stored object. Overall storage availability depends on the underlying Supabase project plan and limits.

5. ConfigKit Family and Kids features

ConfigKit Family supports household Family IDs and account membership, parent-managed child profiles, child PIN sign-in, parent-approved friend connections, Kids Messages, voice and video calling where enabled, curated videos, family learning resources, learning and storytelling progress, safety reports, and a shared household planner. Depending on the features used, we may process account and membership identifiers, child profile names and optional age or grade details, parent-entered notes, friend and approval records, messages and optional media, screen-time activity within ConfigKit Kids Zone and Kids Messages, curated video identifiers, learning activity, family planner items, and safety reports.

Family Planner items are visible to signed-in members of the same Family ID. Child profiles and parent controls remain managed by the adult account that created each profile; joining a Family ID does not automatically grant another adult access to another member's child profiles or parent controls. Screen-time reporting covers activity inside ConfigKit Kids Zone and Kids Messages only. It does not monitor device-wide usage or other apps. Parents and carers should use child-facing features with appropriate supervision and review the information they choose to add.

Family IDs and child friend codes are invitation credentials. Anyone who obtains a code may be able to use it to join a family or request a child friendship, subject to the controls described in the product. Share these codes carefully. Family data is stored using Supabase services and access is controlled by the relevant account, family membership and feature-specific permissions.

6. Accounts, authentication and security

Account authentication is provided through Supabase Auth. Depending on the options enabled and selected by you, sign-in may use email/password or an external identity provider such as GitHub, Microsoft or another provider offered on the sign-in page. Supabase and the selected provider process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Account security features may include authenticator-app codes (TOTP), passkeys, one-time recovery codes and trusted-device records. These features require processing security-related information so that sign-in and verification can work. Passkeys use public-key credentials; authenticator and recovery-code features involve verification data. We do not ask you to send us your private passkey key or your authenticator's current secret code.

Keep your account credentials and recovery codes confidential. If you believe your account or a trusted device has been compromised, secure your identity-provider account, revoke sessions or trusted devices where those controls are available, and contact the site operator.

7. Why we use information

We may use information to:

  • operate, provide and maintain the website and its features;
  • create and administer accounts and authenticate users;
  • protect accounts, verify sessions, detect abuse and maintain site security;
  • store or display content you choose to submit, according to the feature's visibility settings;
  • receive, manage and respond to private support requests and display their status to the account that submitted them;
  • troubleshoot, improve and maintain the service; and
  • comply with legal obligations and protect our rights and the rights of others.

8. Disclosure to service providers and others

We use third-party providers to host and operate the site and its features. These may include:

  • GitHub Pages and GitHub: website hosting, source-code hosting and related infrastructure;
  • Supabase: authentication, database, session and application services, including storage of support requests; and
  • Identity providers: providers you select when signing in, which may receive and return authentication information.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to provide a feature you request, with your consent, to comply with law or a lawful request, or to protect users, the service or another person's rights. We do not sell personal information. We do not knowingly use the site to run advertising trackers or behavioural advertising. If this changes, we will update this policy.

9. Overseas handling

Our hosting, authentication, database and identity-provider services may process or store information in Australia or overseas, depending on their infrastructure, settings and the provider you use. We cannot promise that all information will remain in Australia. Where the Australian Privacy Act applies, we take reasonable steps required by that Act in relation to overseas disclosures. Please also review the privacy information of GitHub, Supabase and any identity provider you choose.

10. Cookies, browser storage and logs

Authentication and site features may use cookies, local storage or similar browser technologies to maintain sessions, remember settings and support security. Your browser may also retain cached pages or downloaded files. Hosting and service providers may create operational and security logs. You can manage browser storage through your browser settings, but clearing it may sign you out or affect site functionality.

11. Security and retention

We and our service providers use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain security and records, or to meet legal requirements. Actual retention periods may also depend on provider settings, backups and logs.

If a data breach involving personal information occurs, we will assess and respond to it in accordance with applicable legal requirements, including the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme where it applies.

12. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose whether to create an account, which optional profile information to provide, which identity provider to use, and whether to submit content to online features. You may stop using the site at any time.

Support requests are private to the signed-in account that submitted them through the portal. The site operator may review requests manually and update request status or add a response for handling your enquiry. ConfigKit Notifications can show an in-app alert when a support response or status change is recorded. The current system does not send automatic support or general notification emails, browser push notifications, or SMS. Do not include passwords, authentication secrets, financial credentials or other unnecessary sensitive information. The portal currently accepts text requests only and does not provide file attachments.

Notifications are stored in the ConfigKit database and are visible only to the account they are addressed to. You can mark them as read, dismiss them, and change optional in-app notification categories in Notification preferences. Turning off in-app notifications does not disable messages themselves or account-security controls.

You may ask us to provide access to, or correct, personal information we hold about you. You may also request deletion, subject to legal, security, recordkeeping and technical limitations. Some account information is held by Supabase or an identity provider, and you may need to use that provider's account controls as well. We will take reasonable steps to verify a request before acting on it.

To make a privacy request or raise a concern, contact the site operator through the contact route on the GitHub profile or the repository. Please do not include passwords, access tokens, authenticator secrets or recovery codes in your message. If the Privacy Act applies and you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

13. Children

Some ConfigKit features are designed for family use and allow an adult account holder to create and manage child profiles and child-facing experiences. The adult account holder is responsible for providing appropriate information, supervising use, and managing permissions and approvals. Child-facing features are limited to the ConfigKit services described in this policy; ConfigKit does not provide device-wide parental monitoring. If you have a privacy concern about a child profile or child-facing feature, contact the site operator so it can be reviewed.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site, its providers or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date indicates the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page. Please review it periodically.

This policy describes intended site practices and is not legal advice. The actual operation of third-party services is also governed by their own terms and privacy policies.
