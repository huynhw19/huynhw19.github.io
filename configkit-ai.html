Web search: Optional Tavily search runs through ConfigKit’s authenticated server function. Queries and snippets are sent to Groq to generate the answer. Check source links for details.
Weather: Ask about the weather in a city, or ask for your current location. For current-location weather, your browser will ask permission to share your location. Weather is retrieved from Open-Meteo; location is only used for that weather lookup and is not added to your saved ConfigKit profile.
Privacy and accuracy: Your question and recent in-page chat context are sent to Groq through ConfigKit's Supabase Edge Function. ConfigKit automatically saves successful conversations to your signed-in account in Supabase (up to the most recent 80 messages). AI memory is separate and follows your Memory Centre preference: automatic, approval required, or off. You can review, edit and delete memories at any time. Only the most recent 8 messages are sent as chat context with each AI request. Groq processes the prompt to generate a response; its handling of API data is governed by Groq's current privacy terms. If you enable the optional data checkbox, only a limited snapshot of your Planner items and published assignment titles and due dates is included. Finance transactions, account settings, passwords and authentication secrets are never sent as connected context. AI can make mistakes; verify important information.
