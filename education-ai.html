Privacy and accuracy: Your question and recent in-page chat context are sent to Groq through ConfigKit's Supabase Edge Function. ConfigKit does not save this chat history. Groq processes the prompt to generate a response; its handling of API data is governed by Groq's current privacy terms. If you enable the optional Education context checkbox, only a limited snapshot of published lesson plans and assessments visible to your signed-in account under database permissions is included. Student names, contact details, grades and answer keys are not requested as connected context. AI can make mistakes; check important learning and curriculum information with a teacher or trusted source.
