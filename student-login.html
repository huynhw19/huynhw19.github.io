Student sign in

If your teacher added you to ConfigKit, use the same email address they entered for you.

  1. The first time, choose Sign up on the sign-in page and create an account with that email.
  2. Confirm your email using the link sent to your inbox.
  3. After confirmation, return and log in. Your teacher-created student record will be linked automatically when the student login setup has been applied.

A teacher cannot see or set your password. If you already have an account for that email, simply log in.
