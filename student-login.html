Student sign in

Ask your teacher for the Student ID shown beside your name in their ConfigKit Education Dashboard. You must also sign up using the exact email address your teacher entered for you.

  1. Open the sign-in page and choose Sign up.
  2. Choose Education account, enter your school, and select I am a student.
  3. Enter the Student ID your teacher gave you, then create your account using your teacher-registered email address. Date of birth and gender are optional profile details; you may leave them blank.
  4. After creating the account, follow the on-screen email-verification reminder. Check your inbox and spam/junk folder for the confirmation email. If it does not arrive, complete the human-verification challenge on the sign-in page and choose Resend confirmation email.
  5. Confirm your email. Your student account is linked to the teacher's record only when both the Student ID and email match.
  6. Return to sign in and open your student dashboard.

If you already have an account, log in with the registered email. A teacher cannot see or set your password. Keep your Student ID private and share it only with the student it belongs to.
