Getting started with ConfigKit at work

A quick guide to creating and safely sharing configuration profiles with your team.

  1. Build a profile with the Mobileconfig Studio, or choose a focused Wi-Fi, DNS, or web clip builder.
  2. Inspect the profile and review its settings before sharing or installing it. Use examples and safety guidance to understand payloads.
  3. Create or join a ConfigKit group to coordinate with colleagues. Share profiles through your organization’s approved channels and follow its device-management policies.
  4. Keep workplace information and profile contents private. ConfigKit generates profiles in your browser and does not store profile XML or Wi-Fi credentials.

Account note: Work account type and workplace name are self-declared profile details. They do not verify employment or provide employer-managed access.
