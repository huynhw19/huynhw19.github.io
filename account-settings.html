Email verification as an alternative 2FA method

When enabled, ConfigKit sends a one-time eight-digit code and a single-use verification link to your confirmed email after your primary sign-in. This can replace the authenticator challenge for that session. Verification expires after eight hours; the next sign-in will ask again.

Checking email verification settings…

Email 2FA is bound to your current sign-in session and enforced by database access policies. Keep your email account secure; anyone who can access your inbox may be able to complete this factor.

Passkeys

Use Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, your device PIN, or a security key to sign in without a password. Add a passkey while signed in, then use it from the login page.

Connected sign-in providers

Sessions

Delete account

This permanently deletes your ConfigKit sign-in account.

You will be signed out and will not be able to use this account again. This action cannot be undone. Any related application records are removed only where your database relationships are configured to cascade. Storage files you own may need to be removed before deletion can complete.

Account deletion is permanent. Make sure you have saved anything you need first.
