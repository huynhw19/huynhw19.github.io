Lock account

Temporarily lock ConfigKit on all your signed-in devices.

Locking revokes all refreshable sessions and sends ConfigKit pages to a locked screen as they are reopened or their sessions refresh. To unlock, sign in again using your usual sign-in method and complete any required two-factor verification.

This protects access through ConfigKit's website. Already-issued access tokens may remain valid until they expire, so this is not a substitute for revoking a compromised password or provider account.

Export your data

        
Download a copy of your ConfigKit account information and personal records available to your account as a JSON file. The export is generated in your browser using your signed-in session.

        
Passwords, passkey private keys, MFA secrets and email-based 2FA settings, recovery codes, verification hashes, active session tokens, and third-party bank connection credentials are excluded. Uploaded files are represented by their database metadata; download the files separately from the feature where they were uploaded.
